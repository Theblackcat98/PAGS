## Status vocabulary

- **Backlog:** Captured but not ready to start.
- **Ready:** Scope and acceptance criteria are clear.
- **In progress:** Actively being worked on.
- **Blocked:** A named dependency or decision prevents progress.
- **In review:** Implementation is ready for verification or review.
- **Verified:** Acceptance criteria and required checks have passed.
- **Done:** Work is complete and no follow-up is required.
- **Cancelled:** Work will not continue.
