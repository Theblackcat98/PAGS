## Status vocabulary

- **Proposed:** Suggested outcome; not yet approved.
- **Approved:** Authorized for planning or implementation.
- **In progress:** Actively being delivered.
- **Blocked:** Cannot proceed without a named resolution.
- **Complete:** Outcome is implemented and verified.
- **Deferred:** Intentionally postponed.
- **Rejected:** Explicitly out of scope.
- **Cancelled:** Previously planned work that will not continue.

## Scope policy

New product capabilities require a roadmap item. Correctness, security,
maintenance, tests, and documentation may proceed without a new outcome when
they do not expand the approved scope.
